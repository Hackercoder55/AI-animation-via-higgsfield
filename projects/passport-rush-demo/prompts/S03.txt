SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE
On the highway the taxi squeezes between two trucks on two wheels and lands back on four once it is clear.
Style: stylized 3D cartoon characters over painted watercolor backgrounds. Lighting: late-afternoon golden hour, low warm sun, long harsh shadows, strong volume and depth. World physics: cartoon physics - exaggerated, bouncy, squash and stretch, but readable.
- Characters and props stay clean stylized 3D; the painted watercolor texture lives only in the background.
- Golden-hour light in every shot.

SHOT STRUCTURE
Single continuous shot, 4s.
Camera: Follow the previs camera exactly.
- 0-1s: Taxi approaches the narrow gap between the trucks.
- 1-1.5s: Taxi tips onto its two left wheels.
- 1.5-3s: Taxi squeezes through the gap, still on two wheels.
- 3-4s: Only once fully clear of the trucks, it lands on all four wheels with a big squash and bounce.
