SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE
The narrator trips on the hem of his gown and crashes chest-first into the tall golden harp while the harpist angel falls backward off her stool.
Style: stylized 3D animated feature film look (soft subsurface skin, expressive faces, detailed curly hair, cloth simulation), vertical 9:16. Lighting: timeless soft daylight, tangerine key light, turquoise fill, bright golden mist. World physics: slightly exaggerated cartoon physics, readable comedic timing.
- The narrator wears a pale blue hospital gown and is barefoot in every Heaven shot; in the hospital he wears a pale blue hospital gown in bed.
- The harp is one ornate golden floor harp; once broken it stays broken (3 loose strings, snapped column, split base).
- Mouths may move on dialogue, but there is no spoken audio; the voice-over is added in post.

SHOT STRUCTURE
Single continuous shot, 6s.
Camera: Two shot, eye level, handheld.
One unbroken take with no cuts: the camera moves smoothly and continuously so the final frame lands exactly on @end_frame (the next shot's opening frame).
- 0-1.8s: He stumbles forward over his gown hem, arms flailing.
- 1.8-3.6s: He slams chest-first into the harp; three strings spring loose.
- 3.6-6s: The angel tumbles backward off her stool, wings flaring; the music stops dead.
Sound: harp music, crash, twang of strings, sudden silence

ANIMATION PRINCIPLES
Apply the 12 principles of animation: squash and stretch, anticipation, staging, straight-ahead and pose-to-pose, follow-through and overlapping action, slow in and slow out, arcs, secondary action, timing, exaggeration, solid drawing, appeal. Movement is snappy and cartoony, with clear holds on key poses so every beat reads.
