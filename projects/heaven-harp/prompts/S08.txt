SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE

Style: stylized 3D animated feature film look (soft subsurface skin, expressive faces, detailed curly hair, cloth simulation), vertical 9:16. - Mouths may move on dialogue, but there is no spoken audio; the voice-over is added in post.
