# I died an atheist during heart surgery and woke outside Heaven's gates

Source: storyboard/storyboard.docx (15 beats, 9:16).

**S01** I died an atheist during heart surgery and woke up outside Heaven's gate.

**S02** I accidentally bumped into the angel playing the harp. CRASH! The music stopped.

**S03** Three strings hung loose. I lifted the harp by its column, and a piece snapped off in my hand. CRACK! The base split.

**S04** Every angel and new arrival turned toward me. The harpist stared at what was left of her instrument.

**S05** "That was the ONLY harp at Heaven's gate." I wanted to disappear. How was I supposed to make this right?

**S06** I had spent my life insisting this place didn't exist. Now they probably wished I didn't exist.

**S07** "Can't someone here fix it?" The harpist pointed to spare strings. "That's not how things work here. Only the person who broke it can repair it. I can guide you, but I can't touch it."

**S08** A family reached the gate. Their daughter looked at the silent harp. "Why isn't anyone playing?" The answer traveled fast. "The new arrival broke it."

**S09** Everyone watched as I threaded a string where the harpist pointed. PING! It whipped my nose. My gown caught a pedal, and I landed on my face.

**S10** "Careful with the column." After a few tries, I secured ONE string. Two remained. Then the gates began to FADE.

**S11** The harpist looked up. "Your heart started again." I stared at the broken harp. "But I'm not finished."

**S12** "Then Heaven stays quiet until you return." One by one, the angels shook their heads.

**S13** BEEP. BEEP. "SORRY!" I woke in recovery. My surgeon smiled. "The operation was successful."

**S14** I reached for my phone. "Calling your family?" "I'm finding someone who can teach me harp repair."

**S15** I had a second chance at life and a broken harp waiting for me at the end of it.
