# Passport Rush - taxi sequence (demo)

EXT. AIRPORT CURB - LATE AFTERNOON
MAYA (20s, ginger hair, blue hoodie) bursts out of the terminal, patting her empty pockets. No passport.
She flags a battered yellow TAXI. It screeches to a stop, bouncing on its springs.

INT. TAXI - CONTINUOUS
The DRIVER (50s, round kind face, cap, huge mustache) smiles, totally calm.
MAYA: "Across town and back. Twenty minutes!"
DRIVER: "Twenty? Take a seat belt. Take two."
She buckles both belts in an X across her chest.

EXT. HIGHWAY - CONTINUOUS
The taxi launches, hopping like a horse between lanes.
Two TRUCKS ahead block every lane. The driver grins, tips the taxi onto two wheels
and squeezes through the gap, landing back on four wheels on the other side.
