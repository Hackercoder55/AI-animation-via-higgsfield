SCENE CONTEXT & STYLE
Kneeling beside the shattered harp, the harpist lifts her face and speaks; the narrator shrinks back clutching the broken column like a shield under the disappointed stares of the angels.
Style: stylized 3D animated feature film look (soft subsurface skin, expressive faces, detailed curly hair, cloth simulation), vertical 9:16. Lighting: Heaven: timeless soft daylight, tangerine key light, turquoise fill, bright golden mist. Hospital: mid-morning, warm blind-striped sunlight on pale blue walls, turquoise-green fill. World physics: slightly exaggerated cartoon physics, readable comedic timing.
- The narrator wears a pale blue hospital gown and is barefoot in every Heaven shot; in the hospital he wears a pale blue hospital gown in bed.
- The harp is one ornate golden floor harp; once broken it stays broken (3 loose strings, snapped column, split base).
- Mouths may move on dialogue, but there is no spoken audio; the voice-over is added in post.
